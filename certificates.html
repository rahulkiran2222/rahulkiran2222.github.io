03 · How to read this page
Learning is evidence, not decoration. The portfolio distinguishes formal education from continuing learning and distinguishes completed records from ongoing programs. Where an institution offers a certificate or record of completion, the page links to the official course or program rather than implying a credential that is not explicitly documented.
