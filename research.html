02 / Research

Questions
worth testing.

Research at the intersection of intelligent systems and real-world responsibility — asking where AI systems fail, how those failures can be measured, and how evidence can become an engineering tool.

Research principle
Don't just ask whether a model works. Ask what it believes, what supports it, and what happens when it fails.

The portfolio connects model architecture, grounding, safety, cognition, language, efficiency, and Earth-scale applications.

02 / Research method

How a question becomes evidence.

01 / QUESTION

Define the failure or phenomenon

Start with a precise behavior, gap, bias, threat, or environmental problem rather than a vague claim about AI.

→
02 / TEST

Construct an experiment

Translate the question into tasks, baselines, controlled conditions, datasets, or system-level evaluations.

→
03 / MEASURE

Look for reproducible evidence

Separate observations from interpretation and make the evidence inspectable wherever the artifact permits it.

→
04 / RELEASE

Turn the result into an artifact

Paper, repository, visualization, benchmark, tutorial, or open knowledge resource — something another person can inspect.

↗
