About

I am an independent AI researcher and founder working across applied AI systems and open research. My research interests center on understanding where modern AI systems fail, how those failures can be measured, and how better system design can make AI more reliable, secure, efficient, and responsible.

My background spans engineering, management, AI data and evaluation work, product engineering, and self-directed empirical research. I aim to turn research questions into reproducible artifacts—papers, experiments, datasets, evaluation tools, and open-source repositories.
