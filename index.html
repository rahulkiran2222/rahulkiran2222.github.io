About Rahul

An engineer moving deliberately toward empirical AI research.

I work at the intersection of building AI systems and asking what those systems actually do. My work spans foundation models, evaluation, safety, multilingual NLP, cognitive behaviour and efficient AI.

The portfolio is organized as a research trail: projects show the artifacts, research shows the questions and evidence, and the rest of the site gives the academic and professional context around that work.

The research arc

Questions first. Artifacts second.

01 / OBSERVE

Find the failure.

Study how modern AI systems behave, where they break, and what assumptions disappear between benchmark and deployment.

02 / MEASURE

Make it testable.

Turn broad concerns into experiments, evaluations and reproducible evidence that can be inspected by other researchers.

03 / BUILD

Leave an artifact.

Publish the code, interface, dataset analysis or technical explanation so the work can be explored beyond the original experiment.
