Archive principle

Keep the
evidence close.

A portfolio can describe the work. A field archive can show the environments, conversations and artifacts around it. This page is designed to stay useful as new talks, workshops, videos and research moments are added.

Publishing rule: use real images, exact captions and links you control. Do not add invented events, affiliations or people.
